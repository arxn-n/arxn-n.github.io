About

From switchgear to stochastic calculus.

My path runs from electrical systems at NIT Delhi, through finance automation at Americana in the Gulf, to quantitative finance at Rutgers — and now back to Dubai, where I build the software a UAE family office actually needs.

My work sits at the intersection of markets, code, and rigorous theory: regime modeling with ARIMA–GARCH and hidden Markov models, tail risk with vine copulas and 10,000-path Monte Carlo, and multi-objective allocation with HRP, Mean-CVaR, and Black-Litterman — across market, credit, liquidity, and operational risk.

I prioritize robust, transparent financial systems that adapt to volatility and let decision-makers act on evidence, not intuition — from the research code all the way down to the Nginx config it runs behind.
