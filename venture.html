A UAE family can hold its wealth across dozens of entities, a dozen custodians, and several jurisdictions — with much of it in private assets no statement ever shows. Holdings sit behind operating companies, trusts, and offshore vehicles; each custodian shows only its own slice. So the question that should be simplest — what do we own today? — can take weeks to answer.

Akeyra is the company I'm building to close that gap: software fluent in Arabic and English, denominated in dirhams, at home with DIFC, ADGM, and the standards a family holds. Not another place to file statements — a way to finally see the whole estate, and govern it with confidence.

OperaFO · The flagship platform

Markets sleep. The mesh does not.

OperaFO is the operating system the rest of Akeyra runs on: one reconciled, always-current view of everything the family owns. Every night, twenty-two always-on agents across five teams — risk, compliance, analytics, data, advisory — read the whole book on a deterministic schedule. The brief lands on your phone at sunrise. OperaFO does not custody, trade, or advise; the family and its advisors decide.

Capabilities

Not a dashboard. A decision engine.

Continuous risk monitoring

Value-at-risk (parametric, historical, and EVT-GPD), conditional drawdown, and HHI concentration, recomputed nightly across the whole book. Alerts before markets open — not after the loss.

Market regime detection

Hidden Markov models classify the book as Normal, Trending, Volatile, or Stressed each morning, with oil-conditional models tuned for UAE and regional exposure.

Shariah compliance, live

A quad-screen against AAOIFI SS-21, DJIM, MSCI Islamic, and S&P Shariah at once, with purification calculated per AAOIFI and a continuous compliance score per holding. No quarterly surprises.

Alternatives tracking

Private equity, real estate, and direct investments — the 30–50% of family wealth other platforms ignore — tracked with IRR, TVPI, DPI, MOIC, J-curve, and cap rate.

Succession & wealth planning

UAE-native succession: Waqf, Faraid inheritance across the five madhabs, and multi-entity restructuring, with 10,000 Monte Carlo paths across three scenarios and zakat calculated automatically.

Automated reporting

A 14-page bilingual PDF on the 1st of every month — performance, risk, Shariah, alternatives, structure — composed from the reconciled record, plus a sealed, time-stamped audit trail.

What I built

Research code to running infrastructure.

I own Akeyra's quantitative core end to end — a 9,500-line modular Python platform, deployed and operated in production:

Six optimization engines

Max Sharpe, Min Volatility, Risk Parity, Black-Litterman, Max Diversification, and Min CVaR — with an interactive efficient-frontier explorer and walk-forward rebalancing over configurable 60–252-day lookbacks.

Regime-aware volatility stack

GARCH(1,1) plus a 3-state HMM (bull, sideways, crisis) feeding 10,000-path copula–Monte Carlo with antithetic variates and moment matching — 7 risk metrics per regime.

Sharia screening engine

AAOIFI-aligned screening across 4 standards and 6 financial-ratio filters, integrated directly into the optimization pipeline for simultaneous Sharia and ESG filtering across GCC and global universes.

Multi-currency consolidation

Five base currencies (AED, USD, SAR, EUR, GBP) with FX-adjusted return attribution and Zakat-aware reporting — built for mandates that span DIFC, ADGM, and offshore vehicles.

Production deployment

Hetzner VPS on Ubuntu behind Nginx with systemd persistence, Certbot SSL, and Cloudflare DNS; per-user authentication and session state.

Engineering discipline

Refactored a monolithic Streamlit codebase into a modular 4-file architecture with isolated test surfaces — cutting iteration time on individual analytics modules by an order of magnitude.
